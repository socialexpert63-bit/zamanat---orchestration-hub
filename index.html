Questions people ask

Does Zamanat hold assets or issue tokens?

No. The hub coordinates the licensed custodians, administrators and technology providers that do. It issues no tokens and takes no subscriptions.

Who decides whether a product is Shariah-aligned?

The appointed independent Shariah advisor. The workspace records the advisor's review and decision as provided, and it never generates a ruling or a compliance score.

What does the AI actually do?

It summarises recorded information, drafts reminders and proposes tasks. A named person reviews every output before it is used.

Can a checklist reaching 100% mean a product is approved?

No. Readiness shows that evidence has been recorded. Approvals stay with the responsible regulators and professionals.

Is the data on this site real?

No. Every mandate, partner and figure in this concept and in the linked dashboard is fictional demonstration data.

Clear lines on who does what

The hub coordinates. It does not take over any regulated role. Here is what stays outside it, and who holds each decision.

What this concept does not do

Act as a regulated partyIt is not a custodian, fund manager, investment advisor or distribution venue.
Move money or issue tokensIt does not issue tokens, execute trades, collect funds or process subscriptions.
Make the professional callsShariah decisions stay with appointed independent advisors, and legal and regulatory decisions stay with licensed professionals.
Declare a product approvedIt never labels a product Shariah-compliant or regulator-approved without evidence recorded by the authorised party.
